## Diff checks

- `View Changes` correctly stayed empty before the first saved modification.
- Unsaved field edits did not appear until the relevant form was submitted/saved.
- Single-piece and collection changes appeared only after the piece or complete collection was confirmed.
- `Expand all` exposed before/after values for references, persons, pieces, sections, metronome marks, and source contents.

## Final state

No review data was approved or persisted. The test review was aborted after the UI journey, and its URL subsequently reported `reason=notActive`.
